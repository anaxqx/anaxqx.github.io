Explore Japan in Oʻahu

When I lived in Honolulu, I used to pass Japanese shrines and temples and wonder about their history. I made this map to revisit that curiosity and learn more about the places I had walked past.

You can explore historic buildings, gardens, and community spaces around Oʻahu, read about individual landmarks, and see how the islands were drawn on a Japanese-language map from 1906.
